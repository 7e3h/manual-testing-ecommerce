## 2. Observed Behavior
During the checkout review step, reducing the quantity of an item (e.g., from 2 units to 1 unit) updates the visible quantity field, but the displayed subtotal, tax calculation, and order total remain unchanged at the initial value.

---

## 3. Preconditions
1. User is registered and logged in as a valid customer.
2. User has added **2 units** of *"Combination Pliers"* (Unit Price: **$14.15**) to the cart.
3. Cart Subtotal is **$28.30**, Shipping fee is **$5.00**, and Grand Total is **$33.30**.
4. User has navigated to the Checkout summary step (`/checkout`).

---

## 4. Steps to Reproduce
1. Open the checkout review screen with 2 units of "Combination Pliers" in the cart.
2. Verify the initial price summary:
   - Line Item: `2 x $14.15 = $28.30`
   - Shipping: `$5.00`
   - Total: `$33.30`
3. Reduce the item quantity from `2` to `1` using the quantity input/stepper.
4. Observe the quantity field, subtotal, and total amount displayed on the order summary card.
5. Attempt to proceed to the next step.

---

## 5. Expected Result
- The quantity value displays `1`.
- The line item subtotal updates to `$14.15` (`1 x $14.15`).
- The grand total recalculates to `$19.15` (`$14.15 + $5.00 shipping`).

---

## 6. Actual Result
- The quantity input displays `1`.
- However, the displayed Subtotal remains frozen at `$28.30`.
- The Grand Total remains displayed as `$33.30`.
- Navigating forward carries over the unadjusted total amount.

---

## 7. Observed Diagnostics (Browser DevTools)

### Network Tab Observation:
- When changing the quantity value on this screen, no recalculation request or state-update payload was observed in the Network tab.
- Proceeding to the next step submitted the previous calculation state.

---

## 8. Possible Root Cause (Preliminary Investigation)
The issue may be related to client-side state recalculation (e.g., missing change/blur event listener on the quantity field in the checkout summary component) or server-side recalculation synchronization. Further investigation with source code access is required to determine whether the issue originates in the UI state management or the pricing calculation service.
